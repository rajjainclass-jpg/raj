Privacy policy

Shop and order information

The RAJ Kirana app uses your account name, email and phone number to manage your account, orders, pickup and customer support. Shop records are stored online using Supabase.

Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your Google account identifier, email address and basic profile information, which can include your name and profile picture, with Supabase Auth to authenticate your account. Raj Kirana uses your account identity, name and email to manage your shop account, orders and support.

The Customer app does not receive or store your Google password. It does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Contacts or Google Drive. A mobile number you enter for shop contact is not verified by Google sign-in.

Online checkout

This website receives a temporary, order-specific payment session to open Cashfree checkout. It removes that session from the displayed address and does not save it to browser local storage. Cashfree processes payment details and may use its own security and operational technology. This website does not collect your card details or app password.

The shop server verifies online payment status before updating an order. For questions about your account, orders or personal information, contact the shop at +91 79875 80921 or rajj48709@gmail.com.
