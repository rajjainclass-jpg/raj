Privacy information

Shop and order information

The RAJ Kirana app uses your account name, email and phone number to manage your account, orders, pickup and customer support. Shop records are stored online using Supabase.

Online checkout

This website receives a temporary, order-specific payment session to open Cashfree checkout. It removes that session from the displayed address and does not save it to browser local storage. Cashfree processes payment details and may use its own security and operational technology. This website does not collect your card details or app password.

The shop server verifies online payment status before updating an order. For questions about your account, orders or personal information, contact the shop at +91 79875 80921.
