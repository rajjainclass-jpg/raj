Ordering and pickup

Raj Kirana sells groceries for collection from its shop in Bamhani, Kareli. Place orders through the RAJ Kirana Customer app. Product availability and prices are checked when an order is submitted. Amounts are shown in Indian Rupees (INR).

Review the products, quantities, payment method and total before confirming an order. Shop pickup is the fulfilment method. Contact the shop to arrange collection or request an order change.

Payments

Cashfree processes online payments. An order is marked paid only after the shop server verifies the provider's payment status. A browser return, screenshot or payment attempt alone does not establish that an order is paid. You can check the recorded status in the app's Orders section.

Cash-at-pickup orders are payable at the shop. See our refund and cancellation policy before ordering.

Support

For any ordering, payment or collection issue, call +91 79875 80921 with your order reference.
